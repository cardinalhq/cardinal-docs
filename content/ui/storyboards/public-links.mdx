# Public links

A public link opens one storyboard that is [shared with your org](/ui/storyboards#who-can-see-a-storyboard), read-only, for anyone who has the link: its investigation and any finished acts, as they were when the link was made. Readers need no Cardinal account. Use it to share a write-up with a vendor, a customer, or a team that isn't in your Cardinal org. Members of your org don't need one: they can open the storyboard's `view_url`.

On a self-hosted Cardinal UI, where links point depends on your operator's settings; see [Self-hosted setup](/ui/storyboards/self-hosted#public-links).

## Rules

- **Shared with your org first.** Only a storyboard that everyone in your org can see gets a public link. A private storyboard is refused with `storyboard_private`: share it with your org first (**Share → Who can see this**). Making a storyboard private again revokes its live links at once, and sharing it with the org again doesn't bring them back. See [Who can see a storyboard](/ui/storyboards#who-can-see-a-storyboard).
- **No story needed.** A link can share an investigation that has no published act yet. Readers see its timeline, and they see scenes only from published [acts](/ui/storyboards#acts). A draft act is never shown, and published acts are immutable, so the scenes a link shows are exactly what passed publish.
- **A link pins what it shows.** A new link shows every act published when it is created and the investigation timeline as of that moment. It never follows later changes on its own: **Update link** moves it forward, and publishing a new act asks whether to show that act. A link that includes raw evidence needs a separate confirmation. See [Links, new acts and new events](#links-new-acts-and-new-events).
- **Your org must allow it.** The org setting **Public storyboard links** decides whether members can create links. See [Org policy](#org-policy).
- **The URL is the credential.** Cardinal stores only a hash of it, so the URL is shown once, when the link is created. A lost URL can't be recovered: revoke the link and create another.
- **Revocable.** Revoking a link stops it working within a minute (a reader's browser may keep showing a cached copy for up to 60 seconds). Deleting the storyboard ends its links too.
- **Optional expiry.** An expired link behaves like a revoked one.
- **Raw evidence is chosen once.** Whether readers can open raw results is fixed when the link is created. To change it, revoke the link and create a new one, so a URL you already sent never starts showing more.

On Cardinal Cloud, public links are served from `share.cardinalhq.io`, a separate host that sets no cookies, sends no referrer, and asks search engines not to index its pages.

## Create a link

For a live investigation draft, ask your agent to publish the storyboard. Its skill updates the scenes, renders and refines previews, then publishes. A storyboard stays private to its author until it is shared with your org or its first act is published. If you publish an already authored visualization through the UI instead, first choose **Publish…**. If the question or time window is not yet stated, review the publishing details, choose the period this version covers, and select **Save publishing details**. This saves the framing while keeping the Storyboard private. If scenes are missing or outdated, ask your investigating agent to update and preview the visualization in its session. Then choose **Publish**. Publishing freezes that act; it does not create an external link.

**In Cardinal:** open the storyboard (on either its Investigation or its Story tab) and choose **Share** in its header. If it's private, choose **Everyone in your organization** under **Who can see this** first. Then, under public links:

1. Leave **Include raw evidence** off unless readers need the raw results (see [What readers see](#what-readers-see)).
2. Pick when the link **Expires**: never, or after 1, 7, 30 or 90 days.
3. Choose **Create public link**, then **Copy**. The link is shown only once.

**From Claude:** ask it to share the storyboard publicly. Claude calls `storyboard__share` with `action: "create"` and hands you the `public_url`. It can set `expires_in_days` (1 to 365), and turns on `include_raw_evidence` only if you ask. After publishing, Claude's publish result reminds it that `storyboard__share` exists.

## List and revoke links

The **Share** dialog lists every link of the storyboard with its status (`active`, `revoked` or `expired`), whether it includes raw evidence, its expiry, how many times it was viewed, and what it shows: for example *Shows acts 1–1 of 2*, or *Shows the investigation only* for a link made before any act was published. *Newer investigation events are not on this link* means the investigation has moved on since the link was made or last updated. Choose **Revoke**, then **Confirm revoke**, to end one.

From Claude, `storyboard__share` with `action: "list"` returns the same metadata, including each link's `through_act` and `through_event_seq` and the investigation's `timeline_head`, and `action: "revoke"` with a `share_id` (`shr_…`) ends a link. Revoking is safe to repeat. Neither the list nor anything else ever returns a link's URL again.

Creating, listing, updating and revoking links needs the **Member** or **Owner** role.

## Links, new acts and new events

A link records what it shows: the last act, its `through_act` (`0` when it shows no act), and the last investigation event, its `through_event_seq`. When Claude publishes a later act or records more of the investigation, existing links don't show it until someone decides they should, so a URL you already sent never starts showing content you haven't seen.

```
act 1 published ── link created (shows act 1)
act 2 published ── public_links: "keep"   → the link still shows act 1
                └─ public_links: "extend" → the link shows acts 1–2
                   (raw-evidence link over raw bindings: also confirm_raw_evidence, after the person says yes)
```

- **Publishing asks.** Publishing an act (act 1 too, when a link was made before it) onto a storyboard with live links is refused with `public_links_decision_required`, which lists the links, until the publish says `public_links: "extend"` (show the new act) or `"keep"` (links keep showing the acts they show now). The Cardinal plugin asks you, unless you already told Claude to update what you shared. A storyboard with no live links publishes without the question.
- **Extending is sharing.** `extend` needs permission to share storyboards publicly, as creating a link does; without it the publish is refused with `share_permission_required`, and `keep` still works.
- **Raw evidence needs its own yes.** When a link includes raw evidence and the acts it would newly show bind whole results or every row of a dataset, or the investigation events it would newly show cite receipts it doesn't show yet, updating it is refused with `raw_evidence_confirmation_required`, which lists those bindings and receipts, until the call adds `confirm_raw_evidence: true`. The plugin always asks you before confirming raw evidence on an extended link, even when you asked it to update what you shared, and only your yes sends it.
- **Links without raw evidence stay without it.** On a link without raw evidence, a newly shown scene that binds a whole result or every row of a dataset is listed, but its Canvas isn't served to readers. The publish result names those scenes in `raw_scenes_hidden`.
- **Update link.** In the Share dialog, choose **Update link** on a link, or **Update link to act *n*** when there is a later act too. It moves the link to the latest act and the latest investigation event. A raw-evidence link shows what it would newly expose first, with **Confirm raw evidence and update link**. From Claude, `storyboard__share` with `action: "extend"` and the link's `share_id`: with no other options both move to the latest, and `through_act` or `through_event_seq` moves only that one. A link can't be narrowed: to show less, revoke it and create another.
- **Reopening an act narrows links.** Reopening act *n* for changes makes links that showed it show acts 1 to *n*−1. Reopening act 1 leaves them showing the investigation only, and the links stay live.
- **No notice of later changes.** A reader of a link sees what it shows, and nothing says that later acts or events exist. A draft act is never shown.

## What readers see

A public link has the same **Investigation** and **Story** tabs as the storyboard, read-only. It opens on **Story** when the link shows an act, and on **Investigation** otherwise.

**Investigation** shows the investigation as of the link: the hypotheses, experiments, findings, decisions and questions the agents recorded, with their text, how they refer to each other, whether a finding was revised or retracted, when each was recorded, and the receipts each one cites. Each entry is labelled only "Agent", "Agent 2" and so on, in the order the agents first appear. Notes, questions and challenges people posted, the agents' acknowledgements, owner decisions, prompts, and which person, key or session wrote an entry are never shown. Investigation ids, session ids and key ids that appear in text are replaced with `[redacted]`.

**Story** opens the same viewer your org uses: every scene of the acts the link shows, with a divider for each act, its statement, state, claims and open questions, its Canvas, and the storyboard's evidence tally with a tier badge on every receipt. It has no way into the rest of Cardinal and no **Open in Explore**. Nothing is re-queried when a reader opens it.

What the evidence drawer shows depends on **Include raw evidence**:

| | Raw evidence off (default) | Raw evidence on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receipt summary: tool, source server, evidence tier, arguments including the query text (credentials redacted), time range, row count, whether the result was truncated | Yes | Yes |
| The full result the investigation saw (credentials redacted), including a failed call's error text | No | Yes |
| Rows of frozen datasets | No | Yes |
| Receipts the storyboard doesn't cite | Never | Never |
| Who wrote it: session id, API key, client, uploader | Never | Never |
| Where each act was written: repository, branch, pull request, commit, directory id, email | Never | Never |
| The storyboard's tags, its metadata badges, and who created it | Never | Never |
| The investigation's hypotheses, experiments, findings, decisions and questions, as of the link, labelled "Agent", "Agent 2" | Yes | Yes |
| The receipts those entries cite, as above | Summary | Summary and raw |
| Notes, questions, challenges, acknowledgements, owner decisions and prompts on the investigation | Never | Never |
| The agent sessions behind it (the **Sessions** panel) | Never | Never |

Public links never show [tags](/ui/storyboards#tags), repository/branch/PR badges or who created the storyboard. Tagging a storyboard doesn't change its links.

The query and its arguments are always visible on a public link, raw evidence or not. Only credentials are redacted: literals in a SQL, LogQL or PromQL query, such as customer ids, emails or hostnames, are shown to anyone with the link, so check every cited query before you share.

The Canvas always receives the values its scenes bind, because it can't draw without them, and the page shows what the Canvas draws. So a link **without** raw evidence can't be created for a storyboard with a scene that binds a whole result or every row of a dataset: Cardinal refuses with `raw_evidence_required` and lists those scenes. Either create the link with **Include raw evidence** on, or publish a new storyboard whose scenes bind only what they draw (a selected field, a reduced value, a series). Scenes that bind narrower values still show those values to every reader, so read through a storyboard before you share it.

Below the storyboard, a footer line reads *Made with Cardinal Storyboards.*, with a **Make your own with Claude** link, and **Report this page**.

A revoked, expired or unknown link, and a link whose org has turned public links off, all show the same "not found" page.

## Link previews

When a storyboard's link is posted in Slack, Teams, a social network or another app that unfurls links, the app shows a small **preview card** instead of a bare URL. Cardinal serves that card for two kinds of link:

| Link | Looks like | Preview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A **public link** | `https://share.cardinalhq.io/s/<token>` | Always, while the link is live. |
| A **member link** | `https://app.cardinalhq.io/storyboards/sb_…` (the `view_url`) | **On by default**, including storyboards published before previews existed. You can turn it off. |

Previews use the standard Open Graph and Twitter card tags in the page, so they work in any app that reads them. Cardinal doesn't run a Slack app for this and asks for no Slack permissions.

### What a preview shows

- **The question** the storyboard asks, as the title.
- **The finding.** The **headline** Claude wrote for the act (see [Link previews: headline and cover](/ui/storyboards/claude#link-previews-headline-and-cover)), or, without one, the statement of the scene that reaches a verdict, picked by Cardinal.
- **The counts and verdict**, for example `3 established · 1 ruled out · 2 open`.
- **An image**: the cover render (see [What the image is](#what-the-image-is)) or a summary card Cardinal draws from the text above.

The description reads `<finding> · <counts>`, cut to 200 characters. When it is too long, the finding is shortened and the counts always stay.

### What it never shows

Scenes other than the one finding, evidence and receipts, drafts, the context the storyboard was written from, its [associations](/ui/storyboards/associations), who wrote it, and its tags. A member link's preview uses only the latest **published** act, never a draft act.

A **public link's** preview is pinned to the acts the link shows, up to its `through_act`. Publishing a later act changes nothing about it until the link is [updated](#links-new-acts-and-new-events), and then it describes the updated link. A link that shows no act has no card of its own: it unfurls with the generic preview.

### What the image is

| Audience | The image |
| --- | --- |
| **Member link** | The act's **designated cover** (the scene the author chose with `cover_scene`), and only when that scene binds no raw evidence. Otherwise a **summary card**. |
| **Public link** | The act's cover (or, with none designated, its hero scene), when the link includes raw evidence or that scene binds none. Otherwise a **summary card**. |

The cover is a render Claude Code made on the author's machine and uploaded after publishing, because Cardinal never renders a Canvas itself. A summary card is drawn by Cardinal from the question, the finding, the counts and the figure, with no scene content. A member preview therefore never shows more than a summary-only public link would.

### Member links

- **On by default.** Posting a member link unfurls it unless you turn that off, for the whole org or for one storyboard.
- **Independent of public links.** A member link preview doesn't need the **Public storyboard links** setting, and turning that setting off doesn't remove it.
- **Anyone with the link sees the card.** The preview's text and image are served without signing in, to whoever has the member link, not only to the chat app. That is the question, the finding, the counts and verdict, and the image; nothing more.
- **The same card for everyone.** The app that fetches the link isn't signed in as anyone, so the preview contains only the items above, and everyone who sees the post sees the same card.
- **No way to find out what exists.** A link to an unknown storyboard, a deleted one, a draft, one whose preview is off, one whose org is disabled, and one in an org that has previews off all look the same to the app that fetches the link: Cardinal's ordinary page with no storyboard details, and the same generic image. A `?org=` in the URL is ignored: a storyboard that previews unfurls the same way whichever org or person posts the link. An app that asks for many previews of the page in a short time (more than 60 a minute from one address) gets the generic one too, and one that asks for many images (more than 120 a minute) is refused (`429`). So a preview can't be used to tell an unknown storyboard from one that doesn't preview.
- **The page itself is unchanged.** Opening a member link still needs you to sign in and be a member.

### Turning previews off

Three switches, from broad to narrow. The org switch and a storyboard's switch each turn off **member** previews only.

| Where | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Org** | An organization owner | **Settings → About → Storyboard link previews**: the **Show link previews for storyboards** switch. Plugin keys can't change it. |
| **One storyboard** | A member | Open the published storyboard, choose **Share**, and use the **Link preview** section's switch, **Show a preview when this storyboard's link is posted**. Or ask Claude: it publishes with `link_preview: false`, or calls `storyboard__share` with `action: "link_preview"` and `enabled: false`. |
| **One public link** | Someone who can share | Revoke the link. A public link always shows its own preview while it is live. |

The **Link preview** section is the first thing in the Share dialog, above the public-links controls, and is there even when public links are off. It shows the exact card a posted link shows now (image, title and description) and this note: *Anyone who sees a posted link sees this card: the question, headline, counts and verdict. Scenes, evidence, drafts and who wrote it are never included.* With the org switch off, the storyboard switch is disabled and says *Link previews are turned off for this organization. An organization owner can turn them on in Settings → About.* A draft has no preview section.

Turning previews off doesn't change what the storyboard's members can open, and it revokes no links. A storyboard's switch keeps its value when the org switch is turned off and on again.

**Previews that were already posted stay.** Turning a preview off stops Cardinal serving it to apps that ask afterwards. A message that already unfurled keeps its card, and apps may cache the image. Deleting the storyboard or revoking a link ends what Cardinal serves, but it can't recall a copy that an app has stored.

## Org policy

The **Public storyboard links** card in **Settings → About** shows whether members can create public links. Only an organization **owner** can change it, with **Allow public storyboard links**.

| Org | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Team orgs, including every org that existed before public links shipped | **Off** |
| [Personal workspaces](/ui/storyboards#personal-workspaces) | **On** |

- **Turning it off revokes every public link in the org immediately.** Turning it back on later does not bring those links back.
- While it's off, members can still list and revoke links, and share storyboards inside the org with their `view_url`.

## When a link can't be created or extended

| Error | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public_links_disabled` | The org's **Public storyboard links** setting is off. | Ask an organization owner to turn it on in **Settings → About**. |
| `storyboard_private` | The storyboard is private to its author. | Its author shares it with the org (**Share → Who can see this**, or `storyboard__share` with `action: "set_visibility"`), then creates the link. |
| `raw_evidence_required` | The link has raw evidence off, but a scene binds a whole result or every row of a dataset. The error lists those scenes. | Turn on **Include raw evidence**, or publish a narrower storyboard. |
| `storyboard_changed` | The storyboard changed while the link was being created. | Try again. |
| `share_host_not_configured` | This Cardinal has no public address for links. | Self-hosted only: your operator sets `SHARE_HOST` or `MAESTRO_BASE_URL`. See [Self-hosted setup](/ui/storyboards/self-hosted#public-links). |
| `quota_exceeded` | A personal workspace's links used up today's views. | Readers see a plain message; views resume at 00:00 UTC. |
| `public_links_decision_required` | An act is being published onto a storyboard with live links. | Publish again with `public_links: "extend"` or `"keep"`. Claude asks you which. |
| `raw_evidence_confirmation_required` | A link with raw evidence would newly show acts that bind whole results or every row of a dataset. The error lists the bindings. | Review them. Only if you agree, extend with `confirm_raw_evidence: true`; otherwise keep the link as it is. |
| `share_permission_required` | The caller may publish but not share publicly. | Publish with `public_links: "keep"`; someone who can share extends the links later. |
| `invalid_through_act` | The act asked for is not published, or is before what the link shows now. | Update to a published act at or after the link's current `through_act`. |
| `invalid_through_event_seq` | The event asked for is before what the link shows now, or past the investigation's latest event. | Update to a `through_event_seq` between the link's current one and `timeline_head` (list shows both). |
| `share_not_active` | The link was revoked or has expired. | Create a new link. |

## Report abuse

Anyone viewing a public link can choose **Report this page** and pick a reason: it exposes private or confidential data, it is malicious, it is spam, or something else, with optional details. Reports are anonymous and go to Cardinal for review. Cardinal can revoke any public link.
